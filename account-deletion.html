How to Delete Your Pin!t Account

You can permanently delete your Pin!t account and all associated data directly from the app.

Steps to Delete Your Account

Step 1: Open the Pin!t app on your device
Step 2: Go to the More tab (bottom navigation)
Step 3: Scroll down to Data Management section
Step 4: Tap Delete Account
Step 5: Confirm that you want to delete your account
Important: This action cannot be undone. Once you delete your account:
  • All your saved locations will be permanently deleted
  • All your collections will be permanently deleted
  • Your personal information will be deleted within 30 days
  • You will be logged out immediately

What Data is Deleted?

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your email address and account information
  • All saved locations and places
  • All collections and organization data
  • Your user profile and preferences

Data Retention

Personal identifiable information is deleted within 30 days of account deletion. Some anonymized, aggregated data may be retained for business analytics purposes, but this data cannot be linked back to you personally.

Deleted information may persist in backup systems for up to 90 days before permanent deletion.
