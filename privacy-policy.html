3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

3.1 To Provide and Improve Our Service

  • Personalize your experience
  • Develop new features and functionality
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the app
  • Provide customer support

3.2 To Create Business Insights

THIS IS IMPORTANT — PLEASE READ CAREFULLY:

The core purpose of Pin!t is to help businesses understand consumer behavior through aggregated, anonymized data. Here's exactly what happens with your data:

What We Share with Businesses:

  • Aggregated trends and statistical patterns
  • Location-based insights
  • Behavioral clusters and demographic summaries

What We DO NOT Share with Businesses:

  • ✗ Your name
  • ✗ Your email address
  • ✗ Your phone number
  • ✗ Your specific individual responses
  • ✗ Your personal account information
  • ✗ Any data that can identify you personally

How Anonymization Works:

  1. Your individual data is collected
  2. It is combined with data from many other users
  3. All personal identifiers are removed
  4. Only statistical patterns and trends are extracted
  5. These aggregated insights are shared with businesses

Example: If you rate a restaurant 5 stars, businesses won't know "Sarah gave us 5 stars." They'll only see "87% of users rated this restaurant 4-5 stars."
